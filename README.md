# Awesome Belgian Law [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of websites, databases and tools for the Belgian legal and judicial sector.

For lawyers, notaries, bailiffs, court staff, legal tech developers and anyone who needs to find
Belgian law, case law, courts or registers. Most sources are free and official; paid ones are
marked. Federal sites are usually available in Dutch, French and German.

This is a list of where to look, not legal advice.

## Legislation and the Official Gazette

- [Belgisch Staatsblad / Moniteur belge](https://www.ejustice.just.fgov.be/cgi/welcome.pl) - The Official Gazette, where laws, decrees and royal decrees are published.
- [Justel](https://www.ejustice.just.fgov.be/cgi_loi/welcome.pl) - Consolidated federal legislation, with earlier versions of each text. Run by FOD Justitie / SPF Justice.
- [Bijlagen rechtspersonen / Annexes personnes morales](https://www.ejustice.just.fgov.be/cgi_tsv/rech.pl) - Search the publications of companies and associations in the annexes to the Official Gazette.
- [Etaamb](https://etaamb.openjustice.be/) - Independent index of everything published in the Official Gazette, by date and by type.
- [Vlaamse Codex](https://codex.vlaanderen.be/) - Consolidated legislation of the Flemish Region and Community.
- [WalLex](https://wallex.wallonie.be/) - Consolidated legislation of the Walloon Region.
- [Gallilex](https://gallilex.cfwb.be/) - Decrees, orders and circulars of the Fédération Wallonie-Bruxelles (French Community).

## Case law

- [Juportal](https://juportal.be/) - The judiciary's case law database: Court of Cassation, courts of appeal and other courts, with ECLI identifiers.
- [Constitutional Court](https://www.const-court.be/) - Judgments of the Grondwettelijk Hof / Cour constitutionnelle.
- [Council of State](https://www.raadvst-consetat.be/) - Judgments of the highest administrative court, and its advice on draft legislation.
- [Council for Alien Law Litigation](https://www.rvv-cce.be/) - Raad voor Vreemdelingenbetwistingen / Conseil du Contentieux des Étrangers: migration and asylum case law.

## Courts and the judiciary

- [Court of Cassation](https://www.cass.be/) - Website of the Hof van Cassatie / Cour de cassation and its prosecutor's office.
- [Hoven en rechtbanken / Cours et tribunaux](https://www.tribunaux-rechtbanken.be/) - Websites of the courts and tribunals: addresses, contacts, opening hours and practical information.
- [Territorial jurisdiction](https://www.tribunaux-rechtbanken.be/nl/links/territoriale-bevoegdheid) - Find out which court is competent for a municipality.
- [FOD Justitie / SPF Justice](https://justitie.belgium.be/) - The federal justice department.
- [Openbaar Ministerie / Ministère public](https://www.om-mp.be/) - The public prosecution service.
- [Hoge Raad voor de Justitie](https://hrj.be/) / [Conseil supérieur de la Justice](https://csj.be/) - High Council of Justice: selection of magistrates, audits and complaints about the judiciary.

## Parliaments and preparatory works

- [Chamber of Representatives](https://www.dekamer.be/) - Bills, parliamentary documents and debates, the main source for the preparatory works of federal laws.
- [Senate](https://www.senate.be/) - Senate documents, including the preparatory works of older laws.
- [Flemish Parliament](https://www.vlaamsparlement.be/) - Documents and debates on Flemish decrees.
- [Parliament of Wallonia](https://www.parlement-wallonie.be/) - Documents and debates on Walloon decrees.
- [Brussels Parliament](https://www.parlement.brussels/) - Documents and debates on Brussels ordinances.
- [Parlement francophone bruxellois](https://www.parlementfrancophone.brussels/) - Parliament of the French Community Commission in Brussels.

## Online justice services

- [Just-on-web](https://justonweb.be/) - FOD Justitie's online services for citizens and companies in one place.
- [DPA](https://dp-a.be/) - Digital Platform for Attorneys: file with the courts, receive judgments and consult official registers.
- [RegSol](https://www.regsol.be/) - Central Solvency Register: electronic files for bankruptcies and judicial reorganisations.

## Company and insolvency registers

- [KBO Public Search / BCE Public Search](https://kbopub.economie.fgov.be/kbopub/zoeknaamfonetischform.html) - Crossroads Bank for Enterprises: company numbers, legal form, addresses and directors.
- [Central Balance Sheet Office](https://consult.cbso.nbb.be/) - Annual accounts filed with the National Bank of Belgium.

## Legal professions

- [Orde van Vlaamse Balies](https://www.ordevanvlaamsebalies.be/) - The Flemish bar associations.
- [Advocaat.be](https://www.advocaat.be/) - The Flemish bars' site for the public, with a lawyer finder.
- [Avocats.be](https://avocats.be/) - The French- and German-speaking bar associations.
- [Notaris.be](https://www.notaris.be/) / [Notaire.be](https://www.notaire.be/) - Information from the notaries for the public, and a notary finder.
- [Fednot](https://www.fednot.be/) - Royal Federation of Belgian Notaries.
- [Gerechtsdeurwaarders](https://www.gerechtsdeurwaarders.be/) / [Huissiers de justice](https://www.huissiersdejustice.be/) - National Chamber of Judicial Officers (bailiffs).

## Regulators and independent bodies

- [Data Protection Authority](https://www.gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be/) - Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données: GDPR guidance and decisions.
- [FSMA](https://www.fsma.be/) - Financial Services and Markets Authority.
- [Unia](https://www.unia.be/) - Interfederal centre for equal opportunities and against discrimination.
- [Myria](https://www.myria.be/) - Federal Migration Centre.
- [Kinderrechtencommissariaat](https://www.kinderrechten.be/) - Flemish Children's Rights Commissioner.
- [Federal Ombudsman](https://www.federaalombudsman.be/) - Handles complaints about federal administrations.
- [Federal Mediation Commission](https://fbc-cfm.be/) - Accredits mediators; includes a register of accredited mediators.

## Tax

- [FOD Financiën / SPF Finances](https://finance.belgium.be/) - The federal finance department.
- [MyMinfin](https://eservices.minfin.fgov.be/myminfin-web/) - Personal tax file and online tax services.

## Calculators, open data and code

- [Statbel rent calculator](https://statbel.fgov.be/nl/themas/consumptieprijsindex/huurcalculator) - Index a rent with the official health index.
- [Statbel](https://statbel.fgov.be/) - Official statistics, including the consumer price and health indices and the REFNIS municipality codes.
- [data.gov.be](https://data.gov.be/) - The federal open data portal.
- [be-legal-data](https://github.com/pieterjanscheir/be-legal-data) - Open data package (JSON and TypeScript) with every judicial canton, its municipalities and justice of the peace, and the legal and commercial interest rates.

## News and commentary

- [Justice-en-ligne](https://www.justice-en-ligne.be/) - Plain-language articles on judicial events and decisions (French).

## Paid databases

- [Jura](https://jura.kluwer.be/) - Wolters Kluwer's database of legislation, case law and doctrine. *Paid.*
- [Stradalex](https://www.stradalex.com/) - Larcier-Intersentia's database of legislation, case law and doctrine. *Paid.*
